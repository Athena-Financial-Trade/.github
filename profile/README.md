# Athena Financial Trade

*A self-funded research group of friends building deep learning tools for quantitative finance.*

## About us

Athena Financial Trade is a small group of friends who share a common goal: becoming quantitative research engineers. We fund our activities ourselves, we are not a registered financial firm, and we do not currently generate revenue. This organization is where we collect the models, tools, notes and experiments we build along the way.

## What we're working on

### Deep learning for quantitative finance

Our main project is the research and development of a deep learning model for quantitative finance. We are exploring how modern neural architectures can be applied to financial time series and market data, with a focus on rigorous research methodology: careful data handling, honest backtesting and reproducible experiments.

## Areas of interest

- Deep learning for financial time series
- Quantitative finance and market microstructure
- Statistics and probability
- Backtesting methodology and avoiding overfitting
- Research engineering and data infrastructure
